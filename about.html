Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you review projects outside Ohio?

Yes. Engineering design services are focused on Ohio, where our principals are licensed. Third-party plan review is provided nationwide and in U.S. territories, against all ICC codes plus the specific state and local amendments that apply in your jurisdiction.

What's included in code consulting?

Jurisdiction-specific guidance on occupancy classification, alternative means of compliance, and code-official negotiation. We help you work through the gray areas of a project before they become resubmittal delays.

Do you handle both engineering design and plan review?

Yes, and it is unusual. Most firms do one or the other. Because our principals ran building departments before founding F&B, design and review inform each other on every project, which is why our drawings tend to clear review the first time.

Do you provide civil site design and stormwater work?

Yes. Civil and site design is in-house, including grading, stormwater management and detention, sanitary sewer and water distribution systems, and land development planning. Specialty needs such as surveying, geotechnical investigation, and environmental work are subcontracted to trusted partners.

How long does a typical review take?

Turnaround depends on project complexity and the completeness of the submitted drawings. We'll give you a firm timeline once we've scoped the project, usually within one business day of your initial request.
