Track record

Design and construction projects

Our key personnel have been involved in the design or construction of hundreds of government projects across Ashtabula, Trumbull, Lake, Geauga, and Cuyahoga counties.

F&B Engineering has successfully completed many Civil & Structural design projects regarding commercial office buildings, restaurant buildings, factory buildings, storage and other mixed occupancy buildings.

F&B Engineering has successfully completed many Mechanical & Plumbing design projects regarding air distribution systems, hydronic systems, exhaust systems, fuel gas piping systems, plumbing drain, waste, and vent (DWV) systems for many different building occupancies.

F&B Engineering has successfully completed many Electrical Power & Electrical Lighting design projects regarding high and low power distribution systems, indoor lighting system designs, exterior site lighting designs, photometric lighting analysis for new and existing buildings for many different building occupancies.
